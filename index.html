NimiCurl Rules

Objective — slide your stones to score in the goal.

Win — first team to 3 points takes the match.

Timer — 30 seconds to aim each turn.

Stones — each one survives 8 hits before it's knocked out.

Ice — one boost per point to help a stone glide further.

These are the default Classic rules — customize them anytime in Custom mode.

Pure Curling Rules

Objective — after two shots, whoever's closest to the center wins the point.

Win — first team to 3 points takes the match.

Timer — 30 seconds to aim each turn.

Stones — each one survives 4 hits before it's knocked out.

Ice — one boost per point to help a stone glide further.

These are the default Classic rules — customize them anytime in Custom mode.

About

Play. Anyone. Anywhere.

NimiCurl is a fast and playful game of ice, physics and precision.

Challenge a friend, plan your shots in secret, then watch both moves unfold at the same time. Bounce, collide, score — or simply get closer to the center.

Two ways to play. One frozen arena.

Dev by Nimiq French Community Members / Powered by Nimiq

Nimiq

Nimiq is a blockchain built to make crypto simple and accessible to everyone.

Its biggest strength is its user-friendly approach: no technical knowledge is required to get started. Creating a wallet takes just a few seconds, directly in your browser, with a simple and intuitive experience.

Nimiq makes sending, receiving and using digital money feel as easy as using a regular app — without the usual complexity of crypto.

Nimicurl is one of the ways we want to make that experience even more accessible: by letting people discover Nimiq simply by playing a game with their friends.
